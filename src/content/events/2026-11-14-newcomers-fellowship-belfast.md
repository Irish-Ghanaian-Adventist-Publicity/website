An afternoon for anyone who has arrived in Northern Ireland recently — whether or not you
have found a church home yet, and whether or not you are Adventist.

Fellowship, food, and honest practical help with the things that are hard in the first
year: National Insurance numbers, registering with a GP, finding somewhere to live, school
places, and getting Ghanaian qualifications recognised here.

Members who arrived a few years ago will be there, and they remember exactly how it felt.

**Bring any letters or forms you do not understand.** Somebody in the room will have had the
same one, and will tell you plainly what it means.
