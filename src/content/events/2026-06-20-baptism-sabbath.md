A full church and a very joyful morning as candidates who had completed Bible studies were
baptised and welcomed into fellowship.

Thank you to everyone who studied with them, prayed for them, and cooked for the
celebration afterwards.

If you are thinking about baptism yourself, speak to the pastor or any elder. There is no
rush and no pressure — studies are offered one to one, and you set the pace.
