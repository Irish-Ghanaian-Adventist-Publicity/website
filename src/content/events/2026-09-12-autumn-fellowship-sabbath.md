Our main gathering of the autumn. A full Sabbath together: worship in the morning, a shared
meal, and an unhurried afternoon of catching up.

**The day**

- 10:00am — Bible study in small groups, in English and in Twi
- 11:30am — Worship together, with music from the choir and praise team
- 1:30pm — Lunch. Bring a dish if you can; come regardless if you can't
- 3:00pm — Afternoon programme, with a separate session led by the young people

Children's activities run all day. Everyone is welcome — members, friends, partners,
neighbours, and anyone who is simply curious. You do not need to be Adventist or Ghanaian
to come.

If you are travelling from outside Dublin and would like to share a lift, let us know and
we will try to connect you with someone coming the same way.
