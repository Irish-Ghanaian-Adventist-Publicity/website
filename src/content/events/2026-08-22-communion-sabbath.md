We celebrate the Lord's Supper together, beginning as always with the ordinance of humility
— the washing of feet.

If you have never taken part before, you are still welcome. This table is the Lord's, not
ours, and it is open to all who love Him. If you would rather watch this time, that is
completely fine and nobody will think anything of it.

Basins, towels and the elements are prepared beforehand. Please come a few minutes early;
the service begins promptly.
