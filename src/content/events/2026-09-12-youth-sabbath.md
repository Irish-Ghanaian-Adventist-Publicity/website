The whole day belongs to the young people. They lead Sabbath School, preach at Divine
Service, run the music, and plan the afternoon programme themselves.

**The day**

- 9:45am — Sabbath School, youth-led in every class
- 11:00am — Divine Service, with the sermon preached by one of our own young people
- 1:00pm — Lunch together
- 3:00pm — Afternoon programme: testimonies, music and open discussion

Parents, please come and sit down. They have prepared for weeks and they would like you
there.
