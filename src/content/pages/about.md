## Our story

The Irish-Ghanaian Adventist Community began the way most diaspora congregations do — a
few families who wanted to keep Sabbath the way they had at home, meeting in a borrowed
room, singing from memory because nobody had brought a hymnal.

Word travelled through the usual channels: a cousin, a colleague, a WhatsApp group. Over
the years we have grown into a fellowship that stretches across the whole island, north
and south, and that still keeps the character of the churches we came from.

We are part of the worldwide **Seventh-day Adventist Church**, and we work alongside the
wider Adventist family in Ireland.

## What we believe

We hold the Seventh-day Adventist faith. In short:

- **The Bible** is the written Word of God and the rule of our faith and practice.
- **Jesus Christ** is our Saviour, and salvation is His gift, received by grace through
  faith — never earned.
- **The seventh-day Sabbath** — from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday — is God's gift of
  rest, kept holy in worship and fellowship.
- **The second coming of Christ** is our blessed hope, and we live in expectation of it.
- **The body is a temple.** We take health seriously — how we eat, rest and care for
  ourselves is part of our worship, not separate from it.

The full statement of Fundamental Beliefs is published by the world church, and we are
happy to talk any of it through with you — questions are welcome, and doubt is not a
disqualification.

## Ghanaian, Adventist, and at home in Ireland

We do not think these three things are in tension.

We worship in English and in Twi. We sing from the Ghanaian Adventist hymnal and from the
Church Hymnal. We keep the traditions that raised us, and we are raising children who
belong fully here — who will pray in Twi at their grandmother's house and in English at
school, and be entirely themselves in both.

## How we are organised

The congregation is served by a pastor together with elders, deacons and deaconesses, a
clerk and a treasurer, elected by the members. Our ministries — Sabbath School, Adventist
Youth, Pathfinders, Adventurers, Women's Ministries, Health Ministry, music and outreach —
are each led by members who volunteer their time.

Our finances are handled openly and reported to the membership. Tithe and offerings are
managed according to Church policy.

## Everyone is welcome

You do not have to be Ghanaian. You do not have to be Adventist. You do not have to be
sure about anything at all. Come as you are, sit at the back if you'd rather, and stay for
food afterwards.
