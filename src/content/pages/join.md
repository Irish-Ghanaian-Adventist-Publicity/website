## Come to a gathering

The only real step is the first one: come to one of our [gatherings](/events/). There is
nothing to sign, no form to fill in, and no expectation placed on you afterwards.

You do not need to be Adventist, and you do not need to be Ghanaian. Partners, friends and
neighbours are all welcome.

## Stay in touch

Send us your name and the town you are living in, and we will let you know when something
is happening near you. There is also a WhatsApp network where members ask for and offer help
between gatherings — say if you would like to be added.

We never pass your details to anyone else.

## Where you already worship

We are not asking anyone to leave their local congregation, and we are not a replacement for
one. Most of our members worship at their own Adventist church each week and come to us for
the things a local congregation cannot give them: their own language, their own food, and
people who understand the particular business of building a life far from home.

## Bible study

If you would like to study the Bible with someone — whether or not you have any intention of
joining a church — we will gladly arrange it, one to one and at your own pace. You may ask
anything, including the awkward questions.

## Lend a hand

We are always short of hands and never short of ideas. If you can sing or play, teach
children, drive, cook, take photographs, run a sound desk, or sit with someone who is
unwell, tell us. See [what we do](/ministries/) for where the work is.

## Giving

Offerings are received at gatherings, and members can also give by transfer — ask the
treasurer. Giving is between you and God. No amount is ever announced, and nobody is
thought of differently for what they can or cannot give.
