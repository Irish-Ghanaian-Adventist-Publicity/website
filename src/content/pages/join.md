## Come and see

The only real step is the first one: turn up on a Sabbath. There is nothing to sign, no
form to fill in, and no expectation placed on you afterwards. Plenty of people worship with
us for a long time before they think about anything more, and that is entirely normal.

See [service times and directions](/worship/) to plan a visit.

## Stay in touch

Give us your name, the town you are living in, and we will add you to the notices — service
changes, upcoming events, and the WhatsApp network where members ask for and offer help
during the week. We never pass your details to anyone.

## Bible studies

If you would like to study the Bible properly with someone — whether or not you have any
intention of joining the church — we will gladly arrange it. Studies are one to one, at
your own pace, in your own home or over a video call. You may ask anything, including the
awkward questions.

## Membership

Membership of the Seventh-day Adventist Church is through baptism, or by transfer of
membership if you were a member of another Adventist congregation. If you are moving to
Ireland from a church in Ghana or elsewhere, speak to the clerk and we will request your
transfer.

Nobody will hurry you toward baptism. It is a decision you make, not one that is made for
you.

## Serve

We are always short of hands and never short of ideas. If you can sing or play, teach
children, drive, cook, take photographs, run a sound desk, sit with someone who is unwell,
or turn up early and set out chairs — tell us. See the [ministries](/ministries/) page for
where the work is.

## Giving

Tithe and offerings are received during services and can also be given by transfer — ask
the treasurer for details. Giving is between you and God; no amount is ever announced, and
nobody is looked at differently for what they can or cannot give.
