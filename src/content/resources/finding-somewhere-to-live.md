We will not pretend otherwise: finding housing, particularly in Dublin, is genuinely
difficult. It is not you, and it is not a reflection of how hard you are trying.

## Where people actually look

Daft.ie and Rent.ie in the Republic; PropertyPal and PropertyNews in the north. But a
great deal of housing never reaches those sites. **Ask in the community first** — our
WhatsApp network turns up rooms that are never advertised anywhere.

## Scams — read this part twice

Newcomers are targeted deliberately. The pattern is almost always the same: a property
that seems too cheap, a landlord who is conveniently abroad, and pressure to transfer a
deposit immediately to "hold" it.

**Rules that will keep you safe:**

- Never pay a deposit for a property you have not physically stood inside
- Never send money by bank transfer to someone you have not met
- Be suspicious of any listing well below the going rate for the area
- If you are being rushed, that is the scam

If something feels wrong, send us the listing before you send anyone money. We would far
rather look at twenty genuine ads than hear about one lost deposit.

## Your rights as a tenant

**In the Republic**, most tenancies must be registered with the **Residential Tenancies
Board (RTB)**. You are entitled to a rent book or written statement of rent paid, proper
notice before a tenancy ends, and your deposit back where you have not damaged anything or
left rent unpaid. Rent Pressure Zones limit how much rent can rise in much of the country.

**In Northern Ireland**, private tenancies are covered by their own rules, deposits must be
protected in an approved scheme, and the **Housing Executive** handles social housing.

**Discrimination is illegal on both sides of the border.** A landlord or agent refusing you
because of your race, nationality or skin colour is breaking the law. It happens, and it is
worth reporting — the Irish Human Rights and Equality Commission in the Republic, the
Equality Commission in Northern Ireland. Tell us too; we can help you write it up.

## Free, independent advice

- **Threshold** (Republic) — the national housing charity, free advice, very good on
  deposits and illegal evictions
- **Housing Rights** (Northern Ireland) — the equivalent service in the north
- **Citizens Information** and **Advice NI** — general, free, and used to complicated cases

None of these charge you. None of them will report you to anybody.
