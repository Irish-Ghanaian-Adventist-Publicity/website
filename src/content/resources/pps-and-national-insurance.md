Almost everything else waits on this. Employers ask for it, Revenue needs it, and without
it you can end up on emergency tax paying far more than you owe.

## Republic of Ireland — PPS number

Your **Personal Public Service (PPS) number** comes from the Department of Social
Protection. You apply online through **MyWelfare.ie**.

You will generally be asked for:

- Photo identification — your passport is the straightforward option
- **Evidence of why you need the number now** — most often a signed job offer or contract
  from an Irish employer. This is the step that catches people out: you cannot usually get
  a PPS number "just in case"
- Proof of your address in Ireland — a utility bill, a tenancy agreement, or a letter from
  a State body

An appointment at an Intreo Centre may be part of the process. Once approved, the number
arrives by post, so make sure the address you give is one where post actually reaches you.

**Then do this straight away:** register for **myAccount** with Revenue and add your job.
This is what stops emergency tax. Overpaid tax does come back to you, but it can take
weeks you may not be able to afford.

## Northern Ireland — National Insurance number

In the north you need a **National Insurance (NI) number**, applied for through
**gov.uk**. You can start work before it arrives — tell your employer it has been applied
for — but chase it, because your tax code and contributions depend on it.

Some people already have an NI number printed on their immigration documents. Check before
you apply for a new one.

## A word of caution

Nobody legitimate charges you for either number. If a website or an individual asks for a
fee to "get you a PPS number", walk away — the application is free and you can make it
yourself.
