Adventists have always taken the health message seriously, and that is more than a
preference about food. It is the conviction that how we live in our bodies is part of how
we honour God.

**What this ministry does**

- Health talks and screening days — blood pressure, diabetes awareness, mental health
- Cooking demonstrations, including how to eat well on an Irish budget
- Hospital and home visits for members who are unwell
- Support around bereavement, including the practical work when a family is repatriating
  someone home to Ghana
- Confidential help for members in real difficulty

**Mental health counts.** Migration is hard. Isolation is hard. Some of our members are
carrying more than they show on a Sabbath morning. Nobody here treats that as a failure of
faith, and we will help you find proper professional support if that is what you need.

If you or someone in the church needs help, speak to a welfare officer or email us. It
goes no further.
