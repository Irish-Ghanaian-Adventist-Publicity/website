This is the work that carries on between gatherings, and it matters more than anything else
we do.

**What it covers**

- Visits and support when a member is ill or in hospital
- Bereavement — including the heavy practical work when a family is repatriating someone
  home to Ghana
- Help for members who have recently arrived: paperwork, appointments, and someone to come
  with you if you would rather not go alone
- A small fund for members facing a genuine emergency
- Health awareness — blood pressure and diabetes checks, and eating well on an Irish budget

**Mental health counts.** Migration is hard. Isolation is hard. Some members are carrying a
great deal more than they show on a Sabbath afternoon. Nobody here treats that as a failure
of faith, and we will help you find proper professional support if that is what you need.

**How to ask.** Speak to any member of the team, or email us. It goes no further than the
people who can actually help, and your name is never read out anywhere.
