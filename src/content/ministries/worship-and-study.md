Worship is the reason the community exists, and it is the shape of every gathering we hold.

**When we gather**, we keep the Sabbath together: singing, prayer, study in small groups,
and a message. Sometimes that is led by one of our own members, sometimes by a visiting
pastor. It is unhurried, and it usually runs longer than planned.

**Between gatherings**, a midweek study and prayer call keeps people connected — especially
members living far from the cities, for whom this is often the main point of contact with
the community from one month to the next.

We study in English and in Twi. If your Twi has gone rusty, come anyway; half the group is
in the same position and nobody will correct you unkindly.

You do not need to have prepared anything, and you do not need to bring a Bible. Just come.
