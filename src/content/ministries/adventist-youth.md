AY belongs to the young people. They plan it, they lead it, and the rest of us are guests.

Saturday afternoons bring singing, Bible discussion, testimonies, occasional debates that
run long, and a great deal of laughing. Beyond that there are socials, outreach days,
sports, and trips with Adventist young people from other congregations across the island.

**Growing up Ghanaian, Adventist and Irish** is its own particular experience — the pull
between what home expects and what school expects, faith held among friends who mostly do
not share it. AY is a room where that can be said out loud without anyone panicking.

Young people from about fifteen upwards are welcome, whether or not their families attend.
