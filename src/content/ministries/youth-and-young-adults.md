This part belongs to the young people. They plan it, they lead it, and the rest of us are
guests.

At gatherings they take a session of their own — singing, discussion, testimonies, and
debates that reliably run over time. They also organise their own socials, outreach days
and trips, sometimes with young Adventists from other communities across the island.

**Growing up Ghanaian, Adventist and Irish** is a particular experience: the pull between
what home expects and what school expects, and holding a faith among friends who mostly do
not share it. This is a space where that can be said out loud without anyone panicking.

Young people from around fifteen upwards are welcome, whether or not their families are
involved.
