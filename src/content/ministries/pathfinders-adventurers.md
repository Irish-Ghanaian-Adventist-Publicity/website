**Adventurers** is for younger children, roughly ages six to nine — crafts, stories, simple
honours, and their families alongside them.

**Pathfinders** takes it from around ten to fifteen. Drill and marching, camping, first
aid, knots, nature study, and a long list of honours to earn. Children finish a Pathfinder
year able to do things that surprise their parents.

Underneath the badges, the point is character: turning up on time, keeping a promise,
looking after the person beside you, and knowing what you believe and why.

Children do not need to be members of the church to join. Parents are always welcome to
stay, and honestly we could use the help.
