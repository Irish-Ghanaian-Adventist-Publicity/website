Every gathering has something running for the children — stories, activities, songs and
plenty of noise. They are never an afterthought, and no one minds if they are loud.

**Language matters to us.** Parents have asked repeatedly for their children to grow up
able to speak Twi and Ga, not only understand it. We are building that in gradually:
songs, memory verses and simple conversation at gatherings, with the ambition of proper
sessions once we have enough volunteers to teach.

If you can teach, or simply sit with a group of small children for an hour, please say so.
This is where we are most often short of hands.

Children do not need to be Adventist, or Ghanaian, to be welcome. Parents are always
welcome to stay.
