Anyone who has sat through a Ghanaian Adventist service knows the singing is not
background music. It is a substantial part of the worship, and it is loud.

We sing from the **Church Hymnal** and from the **Ghanaian Adventist hymnal**, in English
and in Twi, in four parts wherever we can manage it. The praise team leads the opening of
Divine Service; the choir carries the special items; and the drums, keyboard and whatever
else has turned up that week hold it all together.

**You can join.** You do not need to read music. You need to come to rehearsal, listen to
the part next to you, and be willing to be corrected. That is genuinely all.

Instrumentalists are always in demand — particularly anyone who can play keys.
