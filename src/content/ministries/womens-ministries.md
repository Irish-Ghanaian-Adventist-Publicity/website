Women's Ministries holds a great deal of this church together, most of it without any
announcement being made about it.

Monthly meetings for prayer and study. An annual retreat. Mentoring for younger women, and
for new mothers a network that arrives with food before anyone has to ask.

There is also frank conversation about the things that are hard to raise elsewhere:
marriage, raising children between two cultures, work, money, and the particular exhaustion
of holding a family together in a country that is not the one you grew up in.

All women of the church and their friends are welcome, married or not, whatever age.
