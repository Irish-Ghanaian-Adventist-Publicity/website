Anyone who has sat through a Ghanaian Adventist service knows the singing is not background
music. It is a substantial part of the worship, and it is loud.

We sing from the Church Hymnal and from the Ghanaian Adventist hymnal, in English and in
Twi, in four parts wherever we can manage it. A praise team opens the worship, the choir
carries the special items, and the keyboard, drums and whatever else has turned up hold it
together.

**You can join.** You do not need to read music. You need to come to the rehearsal, listen
to the part beside you, and be willing to be corrected. That is genuinely all.

Instrumentalists are always in demand, particularly anyone who can play keys.
