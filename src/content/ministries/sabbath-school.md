Sabbath School is where the week's study happens together. We work through the quarterly
lesson, but the discussion goes where the room takes it — and the room is rarely quiet.

**Adult classes** meet in English and in Twi. If your Twi is rusty, come anyway; half the
class is in the same position and nobody will correct you unkindly.

**Children and youth** have their own classes, taught by members who have been doing it for
years. Children learn the memory verse, sing far too loudly, and come out having genuinely
absorbed something.

You do not need to have prepared the lesson. You do not need a Bible of your own — there
are spares. Just come.
