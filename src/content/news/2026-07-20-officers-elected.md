Thank you to everyone who attended the business meeting, and to the members who travelled
from Cork, Galway and Belfast to be there in person.

The nominating committee's report was presented and accepted, and officers were elected for
the coming term. You can see who serves in each role on the [about page](/about/).

Three priorities were agreed by the membership:

1. **A real presence beyond Dublin.** Too much of what we do happens inside the M50. We
   have committed to regular fellowship days in the north and west.
2. **Strengthening the welfare fund**, so that no member faces a bereavement or an
   emergency alone.
3. **Twi and Ga classes for our children**, taught on Sabbath afternoons. Several parents
   asked for this and we are looking for volunteers who could teach.

If any of that is something you could help with, [get in touch](/contact/).
