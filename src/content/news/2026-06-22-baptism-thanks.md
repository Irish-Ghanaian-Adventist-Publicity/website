Saturday was one of those Sabbaths that stays with a congregation.

Thank you to everyone who gave months to studying with our candidates — often on weekday
evenings after long shifts, often in kitchens rather than in church. That work is invisible
most of the year, and it is the reason we had the morning we had.

Thank you also to the deaconesses and deacons who prepared everything, to the choir, and to
the members who cooked for the celebration afterwards.

**If you are considering baptism**, or you simply want to study the Bible properly with
someone, speak to me or to any elder. Studies are one to one, entirely at your own pace,
and there is no timetable you are expected to meet.
