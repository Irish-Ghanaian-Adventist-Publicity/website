Saturday was the biggest gathering we have held, and one of those days a community
remembers.

Thank you to the kitchen team who started early, to the choir and musicians who rehearsed
for weeks, to the volunteers who kept the children happy all afternoon, and to everyone who
stayed behind to stack chairs.

Several people came for the first time, some having only heard about us the week before.
If that was you: you are not a visitor now. The next gathering is on the
[Gatherings page](/events/), and we would be glad to see you again.

**A note on photographs.** Pictures from the day are going up on our social pages. If you
would rather a photo of you or your children came down, just tell us and it comes down — no
explanation needed.
