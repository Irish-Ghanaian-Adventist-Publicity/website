Thank you to everyone who came to the summer meeting, and to those who travelled from Cork,
Galway and Belfast to be there in person.

Volunteers were confirmed for the coming year. You can see who is doing what on the
[about page](/about/).

Three priorities were agreed:

1. **Gather beyond Dublin.** Too much of what we do happens inside the M50. We have
   committed to holding gatherings in the north and the west this year, not only talking
   about it.
2. **Strengthen the welfare fund**, so that no member faces a bereavement or an emergency
   without support.
3. **Twi and Ga for our children.** Several parents asked for this. We are looking for
   volunteers who could teach, even occasionally.

If any of that is something you could help with, [get in touch](/contact/).
