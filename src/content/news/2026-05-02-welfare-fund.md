Members have asked how the welfare fund works and who gets to know about it. The answer is:
it is simple, and almost nobody.

**How it works**

Speak to a welfare officer, an elder, or the pastor — whoever you feel easiest with. Say
what has happened and roughly what would help. A small group decides, usually within a few
days.

**What it commonly covers**

Repatriation and funeral costs when a member loses someone at home; a sudden loss of
income; an unexpected medical bill; emergency travel; help getting through a difficult
month.

**What we will not do**

We will not discuss your request at a business meeting. We will not read your name from
the pulpit. We will not ask you to explain your circumstances to anyone who does not need
to know.

Asking for help is not a lack of faith, and it is not a thing to be ashamed of. This fund
exists because members give into it, and it exists precisely for the week you are having.
